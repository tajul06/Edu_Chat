Class 9 Chemistry – Question Set (Based on NCTB 2022 English Version)

Conceptual & Short Answer Questions
1. What is chemistry?  
2. Describe the historical development of chemistry from ancient to modern times.  
3. How does chemistry relate to biology?  
4. Explain the importance of studying chemistry in daily life.  
5. What is meant by “scope of chemistry”? Give examples.  
6. Describe the safety precautions one must follow in a chemistry laboratory.  
7. What is research in chemistry? Briefly describe the steps of a scientific investigation.  
8. Explain with an example how chemical reactions are involved in food preservation.  
9. How does excess use of fertilizers and insecticides affect the environment?  
10. Why is chemistry called a central science?

Process-Based & Descriptive Questions
11. Describe an experiment to investigate whether dissolving ammonium chloride in water is an endothermic or exothermic process.  
12. What is diffusion? Describe the process of diffusion in solids, liquids, and gases with examples.  
13. Define and explain the three states of matter with at least two properties of each.  
14. What is the kinetic theory of particles? How does it explain the three states of matter?  
15. Describe the process of sublimation with an experiment.  
16. Explain the process of melting and boiling using a heating curve.  
17. What is distillation? Describe its principle with an example.  
18. How can you experimentally determine the melting point of a substance?  
19. What is effusion? How is it different from diffusion?

Structure-Based & Analytical Questions
20. What are elements and compounds? Explain with examples.  
21. State the differences between atoms and molecules.  
22. Describe the structure of an atom according to Rutherford’s model.  
23. What are the limitations of Rutherford’s atomic model?  
24. How did Bohr modify Rutherford’s atomic model? Explain Bohr’s postulates.  
25. Write the electronic configurations of the first 10 elements.  
26. What is meant by energy levels and sublevels in atoms?  
27. Explain the (n + l) rule with examples.
