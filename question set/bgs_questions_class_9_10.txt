
Non-MCQ Descriptive Questions from Bangladesh and Global Studies (Class 9-10):

Chapter 1: The Political Movement in East Bengal and the Rise of Nationalism (1947-1970)
1. Describe the significance of the Language Movement in shaping Bengali nationalism.
2. Explain the reasons and consequences of the Six-Point Demands.
3. What role did the Agartala Conspiracy Case play in the independence movement?
4. Evaluate the impact of the 1970 election on the path to Bangladesh’s independence.
5. How did the Jukto (United) Front government contribute to the rise of nationalism?

Chapter 2: The Independent Bangladesh
6. Analyze the importance of the 7 March speech in the Liberation War.
7. Describe the formation and role of the Mujibnagar Government.
8. How did the student community contribute to the Liberation War?
9. Explain the contribution of professional groups during the war of independence.
10. What roles did political parties play in organizing the Liberation War?

Chapter 3: The Solar System and the Earth
11. Describe the structure of the solar system and the position of Earth within it.
12. Explain how Earth’s rotation and revolution affect the seasons.

Chapter 4: The Rivers of Bangladesh and the Natural Resources
13. Discuss the importance of rivers in the economy of Bangladesh.
14. What are the major natural resources of Bangladesh and how are they used?

Chapter 5: Social Problems of Bangladesh and Their Remedies
15. Identify major social problems in Bangladesh and propose solutions for any two.
16. What is child labour and why is it considered a social problem?

Chapter 6: Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
17. What are the Sustainable Development Goals and why are they important for Bangladesh?
18. Explain how achieving gender equality contributes to national development.

Chapter 7: Our Duties and Responsibilities as Citizens
19. What are the civic responsibilities of a citizen in a democratic country?
20. Why is active citizen participation important in national development?

Chapter 8: The Role of Bangladesh in International Organizations
21. Describe Bangladesh’s contribution to UN peacekeeping missions.
22. Explain Bangladesh’s role in SAARC and OIC.
